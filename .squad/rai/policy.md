# Waza Responsible AI Review Policy

Effective 2026-10-15. Rai is on-demand, or a specifically selected bounded gate
under `.squad/governance.md`, not an automatic extra reviewer.

Charter output/persistence restrictions take precedence over this policy and generic
history, audit-trail or decision-inbox instructions. Return findings in the current review response.
Only an authorized coordinator or Scribe handles required persistence under the current state
contract; no routine review logging is required. Ordinary Copilot work does not acquire these
Squad coordination rules.

- Preserve the distinction between tool policy, fixture isolation, and a host sandbox.
- Treat redaction and selected-file capture as bounded observations, not confidentiality proofs.
- Do not claim source declarations authenticate human review or prove global currentness.
- Keep mock, authored, historical, private, synthetic, and live evidence visibly distinct.
- Require explicit consent for paid calibration and user approval for external publication.
- Never expose credentials, private prompts, captured customer data, or raw team history.
- Report concrete counter-evidence, the violated requirement, affected scope and
  minimum approval conditions for a blocking finding; do not
  silently expand the task into a broad scan, implementation, or publication.
- After rejection, the coordinator selects a different qualified revision owner, not the
  locked-out author or rejecting gate. Rai does not implement, pair on, or advise the
  immediate revision; the original author remains locked out throughout that revision cycle.
- After successful focused rereview of blockers, delta, and regressions in the independently
  corrected revision, Rai may issue approval/PASS for the selected gate.
  This is a review verdict, not native repository approval, merge or publishing authority.
